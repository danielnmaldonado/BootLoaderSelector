8. External tools, fixtures and exclusions
-----------------------------------------
GCC/binutils/NASM, LLVM/Clang/LLD, Make/Bash, Python, OpenSSL, Java, Jsign,
sbsigntools, QEMU/OVMF, FAT tools and the other workflow dependencies are external
tools under their own licenses; see CREDITS.md for roles and primary project
links. Their binaries are not redistributed in these source/EFI packages.
Jsign bootstrap downloads its official hash-pinned JAR into an external cache.
Optional upstream ECC/EOT parsers identify external ANTLR 3/4 runtime imports;
documentation utilities additionally refer to wxPython, Doxygen and HTML Help.
Those runtimes/tools are not bundled or required by the normal selector build.

Public EDK II test keys/certificates are fixtures identified in SECURITY-NOTES.txt,
never production signing credentials. Their upstream notices remain intact.

Unused prebuilt ARM CompilerIntrinsicsLib libraries and Windows EOT Python
extensions from the original archive are excluded. The legacy binary package
toolchain.gcc102.snb is also excluded: matching source/provenance was not
established. Its checksum catalog verifies a separately supplied dependency;
hashes do not establish redistribution rights. See Toolchains/README.md.

OpenCore and Clover are supported external chainload targets, not evidence of
bundled implementation code. Their user-supplied binaries and respective
projects retain their own licenses. See the acknowledgment in CREDITS.md.

Keep CREDITS.md, this index, LICENSE and LICENSES/ with release distributions.
Preserve every original copyright/license header with redistributed source.
